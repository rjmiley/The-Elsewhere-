Elsewhere Story No. 004. Lethum maps places that do not exist yet. The Raven spends twelve years trying to discover whether impossible maps can lead somewhere real.

Who.

A writer who lives in the uncomfortable gap between meaning and nonsense — and finds it hilarious, then finds it terrifying, then finds it funny again.

The work is surreal because reality is already surreal. The darkness is there because ignoring it is a lie. The crude edge is there because refinement is sometimes just cowardice.

Stories where frogs know your name. Deserts that swim. Logic that dissolves in the heat. An ending that refuses to be the ending.

The Elsewhere is a state of mind. You've been here before. You just didn't have a name for it.
